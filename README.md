# 🛡️ RailShield AI
Detect • Predict • Protect
### Preventive Safety Intelligence for Indian Railway Stations

RailShield AI is an AI-powered safety system designed to help identify potentially unsafe situations in railway stations **before** they become emergencies.

---

## Why RailShield AI?

Women's safety in crowded railway stations remains a major challenge. Existing CCTV systems mainly record incidents after they happen. RailShield AI focuses on **preventive monitoring** by identifying unusual movement patterns and generating early alerts.

## Project Vision

* Smart CCTV monitoring dashboard.
* Suspicious activity alerts.
* Station risk heatmap.
* Safe route guidance inside stations.
* AI-generated incident summaries (future scope).
